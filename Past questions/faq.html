Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about buying and downloading past questions

What is CampusTrend Past Questions?

It is an online library of UEW past examination questions, organised by faculty, department, level and semester. Some files are free, and others are paid.

Is CampusTrend an official UEW website?

No. CampusTrend is an independent platform and is not run by the University of Education, Winneba.

How do I buy a past question?

Open the past question you want and click Buy. Enter your email address, click Pay with Paystack, choose Mobile Money or card, and complete the payment. When it is confirmed you will be brought back to our site, where a Download button appears.

Which payment methods can I use?

Mobile Money (MTN, Telecel, AirtelTigo) and Visa or Mastercard cards. The options you see depend on what Paystack offers at checkout.

Is my payment safe?

Yes. Payments run on Paystack's secure checkout page. Your card number, Mobile Money number and PIN are entered there and never reach our website or database.

Do I need an account?

No. You can buy as a guest. Your access is linked to the email you enter at checkout, so use one you can access.

I paid with Mobile Money but nothing happened. What should I do?

Mobile Money confirmations can take a few minutes. Keep the page open, since it checks automatically. If you were charged and still have no access after about 10 minutes, contact us with your email and payment reference, and we will sort it out.

How many times can I download a file?

Each purchase allows up to 5 downloads and is valid for 365 days from purchase.

I need to download a file I already bought. How?

Contact us with the email you paid with and the file name, and we will help you get access again.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are generally non-refundable once downloaded, because the files are digital. You can get a refund or replacement if you were charged twice, were charged but got no access, or the file is corrupted or wrong. Contact us within 7 days of payment. See our Terms of Service for details.

Will the questions in these files appear in my exam?

We cannot promise that. Past questions help you practise and understand the exam style, but lecturers set new papers every year.

Can I share a file I bought with friends?

Please don't. Files are for your own study. Sharing or reselling them breaks our Terms of Service and can lead to your access being suspended.

How can I contribute past questions?

Uploads are limited to authorised uploaders. Contact us if you would like to contribute.

How do you handle my personal data?

See our Privacy Policy.
