Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains what personal information CampusTrend collects, why, and your rights. We handle personal data in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

1. Who is responsible

CampusTrend, operated by Gershon Arthur (an individual trading as CampusTrend) in Winneba, GHANA, is the data controller. Contact: gershonn767@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Email address, which you give at checkout. It links your purchase to your download access.
  • Account details (name, email, password) if you create an account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never stored in plain text.
  • Payment records: amount, date, reference number, and payment status. Card numbers, Mobile Money numbers and PINs are entered on Paystack's page, and we never receive or store them.
  • Download records: which file you bought and how many times you downloaded it.
  • Technical data, such as browser type, device and IP address, that our hosting provider records in server logs for security and troubleshooting.
  • Content you choose to post on other parts of CampusTrend, such as comments, messages or listings, and notification settings if you turn notifications on.

3. How we use it

  • to process payments and give you access to the files you bought;
  • to answer support and refund requests;
  • to prevent fraud, abuse and sharing of paid files;
  • to keep records we need for accounting and legal reasons;
  • to run and improve the website.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not send marketing emails unless you ask for them.

4. Who we share it with

We share data only with services that help us run the site:

  • Paystack, which processes payments (its privacy policy);
  • Supabase, which stores our database and files;
  • Vercel, which hosts the website.

These providers may process data on servers outside Ghana. We may also disclose information when the law requires it or to protect our rights.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use only what the site needs to work, such as keeping you signed in if you have an account. We do not use advertising cookies.

6. How long we keep data

We keep payment and download records for as long as needed to provide access, handle disputes, and meet accounting and legal duties. Other data is deleted when it is no longer needed or when you ask us to delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it.

7. Security

We use secure connections (HTTPS), restrict database access, and keep payment details on Paystack's systems rather than ours. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your personal data, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a particular purpose. Email us at the address above. We will respond within a reasonable time. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to Ghana's Data Protection Commission.

9. Children

CampusTrend is meant for university students and is not directed at children under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
